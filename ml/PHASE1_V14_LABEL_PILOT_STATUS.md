# V14 motorcycle-label pilot and completeness audit

The v14 pilot adds **two visually checked motorcycle boxes** to the v13 training split. The [correction ledger](../data/source/oi_vehicle_v10/train_corrections_v14_pilot.json) records image hashes, exact pixel boxes, and rejected/held cases. The [QA report](../data/processed/public_phase1_v14_six_box_pilot/qa_report.json) confirms exactly two changed label files, 4,173 unchanged images, and unchanged validation/test labels. The [visual QA sheet](../data/processed/public_phase1_v14_six_box_pilot/correction_review.jpg) shows both new boxes. This remains an annotation pilot; no retraining or model-promotion claim follows from six cumulative box additions.

The old v11 review queue now reconciles against v14 as **7 already boxed, 16 other-vehicle class overlaps needing review, and 70 remaining visual-review proposals**. See the [v14 reconciliation CSV](../runs/public_phase1_v11_reviewed_640_candidate/train_vehicle_proposals_reconciled_v14.csv). These categories are geometric triage only and cannot approve labels automatically.

The [whole-image review sheets](../runs/public_phase1_v14_six_box_pilot/oi_completeness_sheets) cover all **290 retained newly selected Open Images training images**: 40 flagged and 250 unflagged by v11. Each sheet displays current person, bicycle, and motorcycle boxes; [index.json](../runs/public_phase1_v14_six_box_pilot/oi_completeness_sheets/index.json) maps image IDs to sheets. Initial visual review of sheets 001-003 found `ca17abd7809bdd0c` on sheet 003: a crowded cycling-race scene with 11 person boxes and only one bicycle box, despite many visible bicycles. It was unflagged by v11. This needs exhaustive instance annotation or quarantine before using that image for training. Other sheets are **not yet human-approved**; rendering them is preparation for the audit, not completion of it.

A simple high-person/low-bicycle triage of unflagged small-bicycle images identified five frames; visual review of sheets 008, 026, 030, and 035 confirms that the ratio alone is not a label-error rule. `d691f019f2848736` shows mostly distant pedestrians and one clear bicycle; `5585a5b5e5743380` includes spectators; `b4a6971cf3f26b0f` is a waterfront pedestrian scene; and `4ddca0d41e9c5314` has pedestrians and a cyclist. These four need no automatic box addition from the count heuristic. `ca17abd7809bdd0c` remains a confirmed completeness problem for exhaustive review.

Do not train on v14 as if label completeness were solved. Finish the whole-image review, record accepted and rejected decisions with image-level hashes, then create a new version containing a meaningful batch of verified corrections or exclusions. Keep the public test unchanged and lock an independent-source holdout before choosing a training recipe. Campus collection remains deferred.
