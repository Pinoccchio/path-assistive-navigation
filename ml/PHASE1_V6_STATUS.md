# Public-data v6 curation and training checkpoint

**Date:** 2026-09-28. **Status:** Candidate dataset built, retrained, and evaluated. This remains an internal research experiment, not an app-readiness result.

The [v6 dataset](../data/processed/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/dataset.yaml) derives from frozen v3 under the [label policy](PUBLIC_LABEL_POLICY.md). The [build report](../data/processed/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/build_report.json) records **85 removed boxes across 79 images** and **37 quarantined images**. It contains **2,713 train, 251 validation, and 556 public-test images**. The [diff audit](audit_curated_diff.py) confirmed zero unreported image or label changes. Previous v4 and v5 candidates and their interrupted training runs are superseded; v3 remains untouched.

Corrections target crossing-button posts and streetlight-only posts labeled as electrical poles; empty or soil-only pots and cut-flower vases labeled as plant pots; and ladders or escalators labeled as stairs. Images with three or more staircase boxes were quarantined pending flight-level relabeling. One mixed validation image with unlabeled fixed stairs and mislabeled escalators was also quarantined. Contact-sheet review covered all images in the 886-image semantic queue, but it did not certify every box at full resolution or every nonqueued class annotation.

The [inventory](../runs/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/qa_inventory.json) checked all **3,520 images** and found no missing/corrupt files, manifest mismatch, capture-group overlap, exact-image cross-split overlap, or train/test dHash candidate at distance <=13. The [pHash screen](../runs/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/qa_phash_pairs.json) found no train/test candidate at distance <=10. These are screens, not proof against all related scenes.

The 12-epoch YOLOv8n run finished successfully. The best checkpoint is [best.pt](../runs/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/yolov8n/weights/best.pt). On the 556-image curated public test, [evaluation](../runs/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/public_test_summary.json) reports precision **0.763**, recall **0.708**, mAP50 **0.810**, and mAP50-95 **0.584**. Per-class mAP50: person **0.671**, chair **0.757**, stairs **0.863**, car **0.781**, bicycle **0.766**, electrical_pole **0.995**, and plant_pot **0.834**. The electrical-pole result rests on only **6 public-test objects** (50 training images, 2 validation images), several near-duplicate pole views, and must not be read as dependable performance. The test composition changed from v3, so the raw v3/v6 scores are not a controlled improvement estimate.

[Failure-case counts](../runs/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/failure_cases_summary.json) at the script's threshold include 138 unmatched person ground-truth boxes, 59 car, 36 chair, 20 bicycle, 20 stairs, and 7 plant_pot. They also include 247 unmatched chair predictions and 11 electrical_pole predictions. These are review queues, not certified false-positive/false-negative counts because some public images have incomplete target-class annotation. The [visual sheets](../runs/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/qa_sheets/) show residual label and model problems: motorcycles annotated as bicycles, inconsistent staircase box extents, a plant-pot prediction on a car, and an occasional plant-pot prediction on a non-pot patch. A bus in one car review image was initially mistaken for a mislabeled car; inspecting the label coordinates showed that its `car` annotation instead covers a small passenger car in the background. The pole test sheet repeats closely related views. Further source-level relabeling and a genuinely independent test are required before calling this dataset semantically clean.

An [FP32 TFLite export](../runs/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/tflite_fp32/model_metadata.json) was produced. [Raw-output parity](../runs/public_phase1_v6_curated_candidate/tflite_fp32/parity.json) against ONNX on 10 public-test images found all finite outputs, maximum absolute difference **0.00348**, and mean absolute difference **0.0000101**. This does not validate detection-level postprocessing or app behavior. The public test was exposed during previous experiment design and is not independent. Campus/phone data remains deferred by the user. Infinix X6853 latency, alert logic, and walking safety have not been validated.
