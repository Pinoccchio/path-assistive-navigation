# Staircase multi-box visual triage, 2026-10-04

The [seven contact sheets](../runs/public_phase1_v11_reviewed_640_candidate/stair_multibox_review_sheets) show all 40 v11 dataset images with more than one `stairs` box (35 train, 1 validation, 4 test). They were rendered from the current labels by `ml/render_stair_multibox_review.py`. Selected high-priority images were also opened at full resolution. The [12-row hash-locked proposal ledger](../runs/public_phase1_v11_reviewed_640_candidate/stair_review_proposals_v1.json) records the exact image and label versions, current stair-box lines, and a suggested review action. It was generated by `ml/build_stair_review_proposals.py`. This is a **triage pass**, not an approved label correction.

The [policy](PUBLIC_LABEL_POLICY.md) calls for one box per contiguous staircase flight, with separate boxes for separate flights. Some multi-box examples visibly follow that intent, such as left/right independent flights in `openimagesv7_train_0451a182e42b1080` and near/far flights separated by a landing in several ROD images. Thus the 40-image count is not an error count.

The remaining sheets include plausible separate flights, partially visible flights, and dark or ambiguous scenes. Reviewers should decide *per image* whether to keep separate boxes, merge one continuous flight, adjust geometry, remove a non-stair box, or quarantine uncertain images. Record the original image and label hashes with every accepted change, and keep test corrections in a separate version rather than silently rewriting the frozen benchmark.

## Versioned training-label overlay

The [candidate overlay](../data/processed/stair_label_overlay_v1/manifest.json), generated by `ml/build_stair_label_overlay_v1.py`, removes one duplicate or landing-only stair box from each of four reviewed **training** images: `pilot_train_IMG_09074`, `pilot_train_IMG_09199`, `pilot_train_IMG_09245`, and `pilot_train_IMG_09282`. It also proposes quarantining four other training images with unresolved identity or geometry. The [overlay review sheet](../data/processed/stair_label_overlay_v1/review_sheet.jpg) displays the retained boxes; they cover the visible flights, though tightness and completeness still require a second visual pass. The builder checked original image and label hashes against the proposal ledger and left source labels, validation, and test untouched. The overlay is not wired into training and has not been used to claim improved accuracy.

The paired [v11 versus prompt-free YOLOE stair diagnostic](../runs/public_phase1_v11_reviewed_640_candidate/stair_pretrained_comparison_512.json) found 98/122 versus 1/122 matched boxes on this reused public set. The flagged test geometry makes those numbers development signals only. There has been no retraining, independent-source test, phone benchmark, or app integration from this triage.
