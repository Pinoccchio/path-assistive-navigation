# COCO small-bicycle review batch (2026-10-03)

This is a **data-review batch, not an approved training set**. The frozen [selection](../data/source/coco_external/small_bicycle_review_v1/selection.json) contains 80 COCO train2017 images, sampled with seed 20261003: 40 with at least one bicycle box whose longest side is 12–24 pixels at 512-pixel scale and 40 with at least one in the 24–40 pixel range. It excludes all 300 COCO train images previously selected for v9 and excludes images containing the COCO potted-plant category because that class conflicts with the thesis pot-only policy. The source labels are a local third-party YOLO conversion and need comparison against official annotations.

The local archive scan found 804 previously unused train images with a 12–40-pixel bicycle candidate. Its size estimates use normalized box dimensions multiplied by 512; actual model preprocessing may letterbox, so these are candidate bins rather than a measured effective input size. SHA-256 comparison found no exact image duplicates against the 4,173-image v13 manifest; visually near-duplicate scenes have not been ruled out.

All 80 images downloaded and passed PIL image verification. Four [overview sheets](../data/source/coco_external/small_bicycle_review_v1/review_sheet_1.jpg) and 16 [detail sheets](../data/source/coco_external/small_bicycle_review_v1/detail_sheets/detail_01.jpg) show the context and up to three source-resolution small-bicycle target crops per image. The [screening ledger](../data/source/coco_external/small_bicycle_review_v1/review_ledger.jsonl) records image hashes and review flags for all 80 images. The visual screening found three suspect labels (`000000430342`, `000000213527`, `000000539793`), five targets too unclear to resolve at this stage, six crowded scenes at risk of incomplete annotations, and two bicycle/motorcycle class-confusion scenes. The source images of the three suspect cases were inspected at original resolution. These are screening findings, not final official-annotation comparisons; **zero images are training-approved**. Detail sheets display at most three selected targets and cannot confirm every instance, class, missing object, or image right.

The official COCO annotation archive was downloaded from the [COCO download page](https://github.com/cocodataset/cocodataset.github.io/blob/master/dataset/download.htm), CRC-verified, and compared with the frozen third-party YOLO labels. The [comparison](../data/source/coco_external/small_bicycle_review_v1/official_annotation_comparison.jsonl) found **all non-crowd bicycle/motorcycle boxes matched at IoU ≥0.95 across all 80 images**. Seventy-two images also matched official vehicle-box counts exactly. The other eight omitted nine official `iscrowd=1` boxes in the converted labels; none had extra converted vehicle boxes. Thus the suspicious visual targets are not explained by a conversion mismatch. They require human policy decisions about tiny/ambiguous objects, and crowded scenes require instance-level annotation review.

Official image metadata and its stated Flickr license are now in the ledger. The 80 images comprise 10 Attribution, 8 Attribution-ShareAlike, 5 Attribution-NoDerivs, and 57 variants with a NonCommercial restriction. This is metadata capture, **not** a legal clearance for app distribution or model deployment. Before promotion to a versioned training set, resolve each flagged target at original resolution, verify all target instances in each retained image, and complete image-specific rights review. Do not treat the 80 as 80 verified positive examples.

All 18 images whose metadata says Attribution or Attribution-ShareAlike were screened again using [original-size vehicle overlays](../data/source/coco_external/small_bicycle_review_v1/license_priority_overlays/000000234755.jpg). Seven have visually useful bicycle scenes and are prioritized for exhaustive instance and rights review: `000000234755`, `000000429759`, `000000126028`, `000000476841`, `000000331386`, `000000070321`, and `000000472169`. Eleven remain on hold because the selected small target is too unclear, suspect, or visually unsuitable. None is approved for training. This prioritization is a review queue, not a license conclusion or a verified positive count.

No existing train, validation, or test labels, model weights, or prior datasets were changed. The next model experiment should wait for a reviewed data subset and a locked independent-source test; the current public tests have already informed model development. On-device latency and alert behavior on the Infinix X6853 remain unverified. Campus capture remains deferred.
