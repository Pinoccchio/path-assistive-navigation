# V17 expanded person-label review backlog

The [960 px audit](../runs/public_phase1_v15_oi_quarantine/person_audit_960_all/audit.json) rescanned all 283 newly selected Open Images train images retained in v15 using the original COCO-pretrained YOLOv8n at confidence 0.15. It found 18 additional images with zero person labels and unmatched person proposals beyond the 109 previously reviewed. This was a triage scan, not ground truth.

All five [new-candidate sheets](../runs/public_phase1_v15_oi_quarantine/person_audit_960_all/new_zero_person_sheets) were visually inspected. The [decision ledger](../data/source/oi_vehicle_v10/person_review_v17_additional.json) records **11 visibly person-containing images**, **4 false detections** (posters, helmet, vehicle detail), and **3 uncertain images** held for further review. The 11 confirmed images were added to a [new 120-image priority queue](../runs/public_phase1_v15_oi_quarantine/person_annotation_priority_v17.csv) and [combined draft](../runs/public_phase1_v15_oi_quarantine/person_draft_960/draft_v17_120.json). The older 109-image draft and v16 dataset are preserved. [QA](../runs/public_phase1_v15_oi_quarantine/person_draft_960/qa_v17_review_expansion.json) checks hashes, zero-person source labels, IDs, and proposal geometry; it does **not** approve any proposal box as ground truth.

The [subsequent v18 review batch](PHASE1_V18_PARTIAL_PERSON_AUDIT_STATUS.md) adds the 59 images with some existing person boxes as separate instance-level review candidates. The 97 images without an unmatched proposal are not certified complete. No model was retrained, no validation/test labels changed, and no app-integration claim follows from this audit.
