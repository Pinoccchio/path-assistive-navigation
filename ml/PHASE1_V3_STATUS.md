# Public-data v3 screened experiment

**Date:** 2026-09-28. **Status:** Twelve-epoch training, public-test evaluation, visual spot check, and desktop FP32 TFLite export completed. The public dataset remains provisionally curated.

The [v3 dataset](../data/processed/public_phase1_v3_screened/dataset.yaml) is an immutable copy of v1 after exclusion of confirmed wrong-label examples, suspicious wide electrical-pole boxes, and training/validation images similar to the held-out test images by dHash <=13 or pHash <=10. The [build report](../data/processed/public_phase1_v3_screened/build_report.json) records every removal reason. It contains **2,739 train, 254 validation, and 564 test images**. The [full inventory](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/qa_inventory.json) found no missing/corrupt files, exact-image overlap, declared capture-group overlap, or remaining train/test dHash candidate at the screening threshold; a separate [pHash screen](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/qa_phash_pairs.json) found none at <=10.

These automated screens do **not** establish semantic correctness or guarantee all scene duplicates are gone. The [electrical-pole review sheet](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/qa_sheets/test_electrical_pole.jpg) still includes pedestrian crossing posts under that label. The surviving public test has only **13 pole images** and **23 plant-pot images**. This v3 run is for debugging the effect of leakage screening, not for an app-readiness or safety claim. No campus data or on-device test has been added.

The [semantic review queue](../data/processed/public_phase1_v3_screened/semantic_review_queue.csv) lists 886 retained images containing stairs, electrical poles, or plant pots. Its `pending` status is intentional: automated filtering did not replace human review. Two HOT-OSM pole-source shards were [acquired and sampled](PUBLIC_SOURCE_CANDIDATES.md) as a future-data candidate; they were not merged into v3.

## Model result and remaining blockers

The [best v3 checkpoint](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/yolov8n/weights/best.pt) scored **0.731 mAP50**, **0.524 mAP50-95**, **0.704 precision**, and **0.685 recall** on the 564-image screened public test set. These values are not directly comparable to v1's 0.702 because the splits differ, and neither is an independent campus estimate. The [per-class summary](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/public_test_summary.json) reports electrical-pole precision of only **0.321** on **13** pole test images. The [failure-case review](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/failure_cases_summary.json) found **118 missed person boxes** and **57 missed stairs boxes** at confidence >=0.25 and IoU >=0.5. Some unmatched predictions may reflect incomplete source annotations.

The [v3 prediction overlays](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/qa_sheets/test_electrical_pole_v3model.jpg) confirm that the model produces boxes, but it still detects crossing-button posts under the electrical-pole label and sometimes splits a pole into overlapping boxes. The [FP32 TFLite export](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/tflite_fp32/best_float32.tflite) loaded on desktop. Its [10-image raw-output parity check](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/tflite_fp32/parity.json) against ONNX had mean absolute error **0.00000952** and maximum **0.00160**. Detection-level parity and Infinix X6853 latency remain untested.

## Current work order

Campus data collection is deferred at the user's request. The next public-data task is a manual semantic review of the 886-image [queue](../data/processed/public_phase1_v3_screened/semantic_review_queue.csv), prioritizing electrical poles, then stairs and plant pots. Keep v3 frozen; record each accepted correction in a separate ledger before building a new dataset version. Define whether the pole class includes pedestrian crossing-button posts before relabeling: the current pole test sheet includes those posts alongside utility poles, so this taxonomy decision affects both training and evaluation. The stairs sheet contains small or partly visible staircases that need box-quality review; the plant-pot sheet shows inconsistent tightness and overlapping boxes. After review, build a new scene-separated version and retrain/evaluate it. [Campus capture](CAMPUS_CAPTURE_GUIDE.md) and held-out campus validation remain later stages. The current model is an internal research checkpoint only.

The [pole contact sheets](../runs/public_phase1_v3_screened/pole_review/) now cover all **120 pole-labeled images**. A separate [review ledger](../data/processed/public_phase1_v3_screened/pole_review_ledger.csv) records **24 visually clear crossing-button-post examples** (3 test, 1 validation, 20 training) proposed for removal of their `electrical_pole` boxes in the next version, while preserving any other class labels in those images. The other 96 entries remain `needs_closer_review`; a contact sheet alone is insufficient to certify pole function or box geometry. This ledger is a proposed correction, not a mutation of v3 labels, and no new model score has been measured from it.

The [held-out label review](HELDOUT_LABEL_REVIEW.md) also covers all 127 stair-labeled and 23 plant-pot-labeled test images. It identifies a ladder labeled as stairs, an empty pot labeled `plant_pot`, and inconsistent staircase box granularity. Those findings are proposals for a future dataset version; v3 training and test labels remain frozen.
