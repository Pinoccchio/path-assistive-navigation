# V18 partially labeled person-image review

The 960 px person audit found **59** new Open Images training images with at least one existing person label and at least one unmatched pretrained-model person proposal. All 15 [overlay sheets](../runs/public_phase1_v15_oi_quarantine/person_audit_960_all/partial_person_sheets) were visually inspected. Many show clearly visible people beyond the cyan source boxes; some red proposals are false positives on posters, decals, or other objects. This makes the whole image a review candidate; no individual proposal is approved ground truth.

The [frozen review ledger](../data/source/oi_vehicle_v10/person_review_v18_partial.json) records image and label hashes, original person-box counts, unmatched-proposal counts, and sheet locations. The [new combined draft](../runs/public_phase1_v15_oi_quarantine/person_draft_960/draft_v18_179.json) starts with the original person boxes and then adds unmatched proposals as editable candidates. The [priority queue](../runs/public_phase1_v15_oi_quarantine/person_annotation_priority_v18.csv) now contains **179 images**: 120 visually confirmed zero-person-label images plus 59 partially labeled review candidates. [Structural QA](../runs/public_phase1_v15_oi_quarantine/person_draft_960/qa_v18_partial_review.json) checks source hashes, original label hashes, box geometry, and queue membership; it does not certify annotation completeness.

The [local reviewer](PERSON_ANNOTATION_REVIEW.md) loads all 179 images. Existing source boxes are included as editable starting boxes. A reviewer must inspect every visible person and remove false proposals; the second reviewer and vehicle-completeness check remain gates before a corrected training version. The 97 images without an unmatched proposal are not certified complete. No training run or app promotion follows from this audit.
