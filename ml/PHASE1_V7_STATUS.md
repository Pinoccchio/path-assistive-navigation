# Public-data v7 vehicle-label correction checkpoint

**Date:** 2026-09-29. **Status:** Dataset correction, 12-epoch retraining, public-test evaluation, visual review, and desktop export complete. This remains an internal research experiment, not an app-readiness result.

The [v7 dataset](../data/processed/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/dataset.yaml) derives from frozen v6. The [build report](../data/processed/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/build_report.json) records **100 removed `bicycle` boxes across 98 images** after visual review confirmed motorcycles, motor scooters, or motor trikes instead of pedal bicycles. All 3,520 images remain in their original train/validation/test split; those without another target label become hard-negative images. The [diff audit](audit_curated_diff.py) found zero unreported label or image changes. v6 is unchanged.

The [inventory](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/qa_inventory.json) found zero missing/corrupt files, manifest mismatch, capture-group overlap, exact-image cross-split overlap, or train/test dHash candidate at distance <=13. The [pHash screen](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/qa_phash_pairs.json) found zero train/test pairs at distance <=10. These screens do not establish scene independence.

Before retraining, the frozen v6 checkpoint was evaluated on the corrected v7 test annotations as a same-test baseline. Its [baseline report](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/public_test_summary_v6_on_v7.json) gives overall precision **0.742**, recall **0.708**, mAP50 **0.807**, and mAP50-95 **0.583**; bicycle mAP50 **0.751** on **52** annotated bicycle instances in 43 images. This is a controlled model comparison baseline only, not an independent test: prior experiment design and this label review used public-test images.

The bus/car concern from the v6 nine-image sheet was checked against the actual annotation coordinates. The visible bus is not the annotated `car`; the small passenger car in the background is. No car label was changed from that case. Staircase extent inconsistency and the extremely small electrical-pole sample remain open label-quality limits. Phone/campus data remain deferred by the user.

The [v7 checkpoint](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/yolov8n/weights/best.pt) was trained from the same initial YOLOv8n weights and 12-epoch settings as v6. On the **same corrected v7 test**, [v7 evaluation](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/public_test_summary.json) gives overall precision **0.730**, recall **0.718**, mAP50 **0.802**, and mAP50-95 **0.574**. Bicycle precision is **0.698**, recall **0.665**, mAP50 **0.743**, and mAP50-95 **0.580**, versus frozen v6 on the same annotations: **0.633**, **0.654**, **0.751**, and **0.574**, respectively. The targeted false-positive improvement came with a small decrease in overall mAP50 (0.807 to 0.802); do not claim blanket model improvement.

At confidence **0.25** and image size **512**, the [v6 hard-negative QA](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/vehicle_negative_qa_v6.json) predicted bicycle on **14 of 16** reviewed motorcycle/scooter test images. The [v7 hard-negative QA](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/vehicle_negative_qa_v7.json) predicted bicycle on **3 of 16**. Residual test cases are `rod_test_IMG_19721`, `rod_test_IMG_19724`, and `rod_test_IMG_19726`; further data or classifier/threshold work may help but should be evaluated on a fresh, unexposed test. The [visual sheets](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/qa_sheets/) still show occasional wrong box geometry and misses in other classes. [Failure-case counts](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/failure_cases_summary.json) are review queues only because public annotations can be incomplete.

An [FP32 TFLite export](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/tflite_fp32/model_metadata.json) was produced. [Raw-output ONNX/TFLite parity](../runs/public_phase1_v7_vehicle_labels_candidate/tflite_fp32/parity.json) on 10 public-test images found all outputs finite, maximum absolute difference **0.00177**, and mean absolute difference **0.00000977**. Detection-level postprocessing, Infinix X6853 latency, alert behavior, and walking safety are not yet validated. No new dataset was downloaded for this correction because the existing public images contained the needed hard negatives; rights review remains required before public redistribution.

**2026-09-29 external follow-up:** A separate [COCO val2017 vehicle QA](PHASE1_EXTERNAL_VEHICLE_QA.md) found v7 bicycle recall **21/98** boxes at IoU ≥ 0.5 and bicycle false alarms on **6/40** motorcycle-only images. The same slice gave v6 **26/98** and **13/40**, respectively. This confirms a false-alarm tradeoff with worse bicycle detection on new public images. Do not promote v7 as ready for the app. One stair training box also needs correction or quarantine before the next curated candidate.

Subsequent audited [v8](PHASE1_V8_STATUS.md) and [v9](PHASE1_V9_STATUS.md) research candidates added an explicit motorcycle class and COCO train images. The newest public-data result still has low bicycle recall and unresolved motorcycle false alarms; none is app-ready.
