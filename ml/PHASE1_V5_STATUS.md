# Public-data v5 curation checkpoint

**Date:** 2026-09-28. **Status:** Superseded by [v6](PHASE1_V6_STATUS.md) after full-resolution review found streetlight posts mislabeled as electrical poles. The v5 training run was stopped during epoch 3 and has no final evaluation. This is an internal research dataset, not a navigation-ready benchmark.

The [v5 dataset](../data/processed/public_phase1_v5_curated_candidate/dataset.yaml) is derived from frozen v3 under the [label policy](PUBLIC_LABEL_POLICY.md). Its [build report](../data/processed/public_phase1_v5_curated_candidate/build_report.json) records every removed box and quarantined image. It retains **2,713 train, 251 validation, and 556 public-test images**. The exact [diff audit](audit_curated_diff.py) confirmed **47 removed boxes in 41 images**, **37 quarantined images**, and zero unreported image or label changes. v3 remains intact.

The removed labels cover crossing-button posts mislabeled as electrical poles, empty/soil-only pots and flower vases mislabeled as plant pots, and ladders/escalators mislabeled as stairs. Stair images with three or more class boxes were quarantined for manual relabeling because the source often boxed individual steps instead of one staircase flight. A mixed validation image containing unlabeled fixed stairs and labeled escalators was also quarantined. The preliminary v4 training run was stopped during its first epoch after that additional validation error was found; it has no usable checkpoint or result.

The [v5 inventory](../runs/public_phase1_v5_curated_candidate/qa_inventory.json) checked all 3,520 images and found zero missing/corrupt/manifest-mismatched files, capture-group overlap, exact-image cross-split overlap, or train/test dHash candidates at distance <=13. The [pHash screen](../runs/public_phase1_v5_curated_candidate/qa_phash_pairs.json) found zero train/test candidates at distance <=10. These are screening thresholds, not proof that all related scenes or annotations were discovered.

**Remaining limitations:** The original public test was seen during prior experiment design, so it is not an untouched independent test. After corrections, the electrical-pole class has only a handful of public test positives and unresolved utility-pole versus structural-post examples. A visual contact-sheet pass does not certify every box. The current data still omits the intended phone/campus domain, which the user deferred. Model evaluation, detection-level TFLite parity, phone latency, alerts, and walking safety remain separate work.
