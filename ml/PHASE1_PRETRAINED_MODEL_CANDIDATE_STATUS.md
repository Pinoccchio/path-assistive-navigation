# Pretrained environment-model candidate, 2026-10-04

## Decision

Keep the existing v11 checkpoint as a research baseline. The official YOLO26n Objects365v1 checkpoint is a **broad-vocabulary research candidate**, not a replacement or an app model. Its 365 labels include useful street objects, but do not include project-defined `stairs` or `electrical_pole`; its `potted plant` category does not follow our container-only `plant_pot` policy. No model has been promoted for navigation alerts.

## Stairs and model-combination choice

- Existing v11 includes a custom `stairs` class. Objects365 does not. Running v11 plus Objects365 on every camera frame would add compute while the second model adds no validated staircase capability.
- The official [YOLOE-26n prompt-free checkpoint](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/yoloe) was downloaded to `ml/pretrained/yoloe-26n-seg-pf.pt` (16,271,345 bytes, SHA-256 `ae805a9ac11c85214588b32da8248d68a516e1d071465e1e86a03cbfadb66ba7`). Its 4,585-name vocabulary includes `stair`, `stairs`, and `stairwell`. The paired [stair report](../runs/public_phase1_v11_reviewed_640_candidate/stair_pretrained_comparison_512.json) used all three as one staircase candidate, and `ml/evaluate_stair_pretrained_candidates.py` reproduces it:

  | Reused public test, 512px / confidence 0.25 / IoU 0.5 | v11 | Prompt-free YOLOE-26n |
  | --- | ---: | ---: |
  | Stair boxes matched out of 122 labels | **98** | 1 |
  | Images with any stair prediction out of 118 stair-labeled images | **101** | 1 |
  | Images with a stair prediction out of 502 zero-stair-label images | 3 | 4 |

  The last row is **not a false-positive rate** because zero-stair labels may be incomplete. At this threshold, prompt-free YOLOE is a poor staircase replacement for v11. A text-prompted YOLOE variant remains a separate, untested candidate; it needs a text encoder download and its predictions still require review.
- A [public student stair model](https://github.com/Yesh492/stair-detection-training) reports a TFLite export and metrics from roughly 400 stair images. Its dataset, label convention, license, and results have not been validated against our images, so it is not selected for redistribution or app integration.
- Preferred final direction: complete instance-level annotation review, lock an independent-source evaluation set, and fine-tune one compact detector with project-specific hazard labels while checking that stair performance is retained. Use a second specialized model only if controlled tests show a material improvement in missed stairs without unacceptable false alerts, latency, heat, or battery cost on the Infinix X6853. Do not merge weights or auto-accept model-generated labels.

## Next evidence gates

The [stair-label inventory](../runs/public_phase1_v11_reviewed_640_candidate/stair_label_inventory.json), generated by `ml/audit_stair_label_inventory.py`, counts 386 stair boxes in 351 train images, 41 in 40 validation images, and 122 in 118 test images. It flags 35 train, 1 validation, and 4 test images with more than one stair box. The [visual triage and provisional correction overlay](PHASE1_STAIR_VISUAL_REVIEW_STATUS.md) found both plausible separate flights and questionable boxes on landings, fountain ledges, railings, and overlapping test stairs. The original labels were not edited. The inventory does not certify single-box images or missing stair instances. The test positives mostly come from ROD (103/122 boxes), so this benchmark alone does not establish broad environment transfer.

1. Complete the [179-image person annotation review](PHASE1_V18_PARTIAL_PERSON_AUDIT_STATUS.md), plus bicycle/motorcycle and stair-label completeness checks. No retraining on incomplete labels.
2. Curate independent-source stair positives and negatives under the [project label policy](PUBLIC_LABEL_POLICY.md), including ascending/descending, outdoor/indoor, ramps, shadows, and step-like patterns. Keep campus capture deferred per current scope.
3. Create a reviewed, independent-source staircase holdout; compare v11 with any rights-cleared text-prompted or specialist stair model on that set. Measure recall, false alerts per scene, and box quality. The reused public diagnostic already rejects the prompt-free YOLOE checkpoint as a direct stair replacement.
4. Export the finalist, validate exported-versus-PyTorch predictions, and benchmark the complete camera-to-alert path on the Infinix X6853. These steps remain unverified.
