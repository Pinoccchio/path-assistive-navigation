# Public-data v9 COCO-train candidate — 2026-09-29

**Status:** Built, audited, trained for 12 epochs, and evaluated. **Research candidate only; not app-ready.**

The [v9 dataset](../data/processed/public_phase1_v9_coco_train_candidate/dataset.yaml) preserves every v8 image and label and adds 300 COCO train2017 images: 150 with bicycle but no motorcycle label, and 150 with motorcycle but no bicycle label. The [frozen selection](../data/source/coco_external/train_selection.json) was chosen with seed `20261001` from a [checksum-verified COCO YOLO-label archive](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LibreYOLO/coco2017). The [builder](build_v9_coco_train_candidate.py) maps COCO person, chair, car, bicycle, and motorcycle boxes to project classes; images with COCO `potted plant` labels are excluded because their foliage-inclusive box convention conflicts with the pot-only project policy. The [diff audit](audit_v9_coco_train_candidate.py) verified all 3,519 v8 images/labels were preserved and exactly 300 selected training images were added, with zero unexpected changes. New COCO train images have no exact-byte overlap with the 240 COCO val images used in three QA slices. Per-image COCO licenses still require review before redistribution.

The [training script](train_v9_coco_train_candidate.py) uses the same initial YOLOv8n weights, 512-pixel input, batch 4, seed 42, and 12 epochs as v8; the [environment record](../runs/public_phase1_v9_coco_train_candidate/environment.json) and [best checkpoint](../runs/public_phase1_v9_coco_train_candidate/yolov8n/weights/best.pt) are saved. The v8/v9 validation and public-test splits are unchanged. On the **reused public test**, [v9 evaluation](../runs/public_phase1_v9_coco_train_candidate/public_test_summary.json) gives overall precision 0.765, recall 0.730, mAP50 0.777, and mAP50-95 0.582 across eight classes. Bicycle precision is 0.825, recall 0.725, mAP50 0.831; motorcycle precision 0.524, recall 0.625, mAP50 0.371. The public test was involved in previous dataset/model decisions and is not independent.

Three disjoint 80-image COCO val2017 slices (40 bicycle-only and 40 motorcycle-only images each) were evaluated with confidence 0.25, image size 512, and one-to-one bicycle matching at IoU ≥ 0.5. Slices 1 and 2 were development evidence before v9. Slice 3 was selected and frozen before v9 training, then evaluated once at the preset settings. All are public-image diagnostics rather than a campus or phone-domain test.

v9 recovers more bicycle boxes than v8 across all three slices, but its motorcycle false alarms increase on the fresh third slice. Even the combined bicycle recall is only about 29%, so no candidate can be called reliable for walking assistance. These slices are small and from the same public dataset; the combined row is descriptive, not an independent benchmark or mAP. The public-test improvement and third-slice tradeoff both matter; do not select a deployment model from one favorable number.

The next meaningful step is a source-diverse, manually reviewed training/evaluation collection that reflects the intended camera viewpoint and includes small bicycles, motorcycles, scooters, trikes, occlusion, and backgrounds. Audit stair box geometry and the tiny electrical-pole sample separately. Keep a new source-separated test untouched during training. Only after acceptable bicycle recall and false-alarm behavior should a candidate be exported and checked end-to-end on the Infinix X6853 for actual detections, latency, and alerts. Campus collection remains deferred at the user's request.

COCO provenance: [COCO API](https://github.com/cocodataset/cocoapi); [LibreYOLO annotation/label conversion and checksums](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LibreYOLO/coco2017).
